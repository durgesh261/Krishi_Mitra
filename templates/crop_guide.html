2. Land Preparation & Sowing Plan

Tillage, compost incorporation, seed rate, row spacing, and seed treatment

Land Preparation & Tillage

1 deep summer ploughing followed by 2-3 harrowings and planking. Incorporate 6-8 tonnes FYM/compost per acre.

Seed Rate, Spacing & Treatment

Seed Rate: 40-45 kg/acre. Spacing: 20-22.5 cm row-to-row, 4-5 cm depth. Seed treatment: Carbendazim @ 2g/kg seed.

3

3. Precision Water & Fertilizer Schedule

Stage-wise watering schedule and farmer-friendly fertilizer bag doses per acre

Fertilizer Schedule (Bags per Acre)

Basal: 1 bag DAP + 1/2 bag MOP + 10kg Zinc Sulphate. Top Dressing: 1 bag Urea at 1st irrigation, 1 bag Urea at 2nd irrigation.

Watering & Precision Irrigation

Critical moisture stages: CRI (21d), Tillering (40-45d), Late Jointing (60-65d), Flowering (80-85d).

NPK & Micronutrient Schedule

Basal: 60kg N, 60kg P2O5, 40kg K2O per ha. Top dress: 30kg N at 1st irrigation, 30kg N at 2nd irrigation.

4

4. Weed, Pest & Disease Protection

Weed-free periods, insect monitoring, and exact chemical/biological dosages

Weed Management & Control

Pre-emergence: Pendimethalin 30% EC @ 1-1.25 L/acre within 48h. Post-emergence: Clodinafop 15% WP @ 160g/acre for Phalaris minor.

Pest & Disease Advisory (IPM)

Watch for Yellow/Stripe Rust (Puccinia striiformis) and Karnal Bunt. Spray Propiconazole 25% EC @ 1ml/L at first sign.

5. Harvesting & Farm Profitability

Maturity signs, post-harvest drying, cost of cultivation, and expected net profit

Harvesting & Safe Storage

Harvest when earheads turn golden yellow (moisture < 14%). Sun-dry to 10-12% moisture before storing in clean bins.

Farm Economics & Profitability per Acre

Cost: ₹14,000 - ₹17,500/acre. Yield: 18 - 22 q/acre. Gross Revenue: ₹41,000 - ₹50,000. Net Profit: ₹25,000 - ₹33,000/acre.
